Strict laws are premature and counterproductive given the nascent and rapidly evolving nature of LLM technology. Codifying rigid regulations now would inevitably stifle the foundational research and development, disproportionately burden smaller innovators, and ultimately prevent the very dynamic advancements that could emerge to mitigate potential risks. Instead of broad, rigid legislation that locks us into current assumptions, we need agile governance frameworks, industry-led ethical guidelines, and targeted regulations on *specific harmful applications or misuse cases*, allowing for iterative development and adaptation to a technology whose full capabilities and societal implications are still being understood. Over-regulation would halt progress, not guarantee safety.
