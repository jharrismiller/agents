The unprecedented scale, speed, and persuasive power of LLMs pose significant threats to public safety, democratic processes, and individual rights, necessitating immediate and strict legal regulation. Without robust laws, unchecked LLMs will inevitably amplify misinformation, perpetuate societal biases, facilitate sophisticated fraud, erode privacy, and potentially automate harmful decision-making beyond human oversight. Self-regulation has proven insufficient for emerging technologies with such widespread societal impact. Strict laws are essential to mandate transparency in training data and model behavior, enforce accountability for harmful outputs, protect intellectual property, ensure equitable access, and safeguard against systemic risks, ultimately preserving societal stability and trust in the digital age.
